Why horses?

Horses respond to what is happening in the present moment. They notice posture, movement, energy and boundaries, and they are always quietly checking for safety. Sharing space with a calm horse can help your own nervous system remember what safety, presence and connection feel like, without pressure.

In the quiet rhythm of a horse's breath, the nervous system finds its way home. The horse does not judge or ask for explanations. It is simply there: whole, present, powerful.

What is Reiki?

Reiki is a gentle, non-invasive Japanese relaxation technique. The practitioner's hands rest lightly on the body or just above it in a series of positions. Many people describe deep relaxation and a sense of peace.

The experience at the barn

  1. Arrive and settle. A short, friendly welcome and a quick intake form and waiver.
  2. Meet the horses. Quiet time near the horses at their pace. No riding.
  3. Reiki. Gentle Reiki seated or lying down, with the horses close by.
  4. Rest and reflect. Time to sit, breathe and notice how you feel before you drive home.

Located in Thompsonville, Michigan, near Crystal Mountain. The exact address is shared when you book. In winter, dress warmly and wear boots.

Questions

Do I need horse experience?

No. You will not ride. The horses are simply present, and Tiffany guides everything at a calm pace.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes or boots. Dress for the weather; it is a barn.

Is Reiki a medical treatment?

No. Reiki is a complementary relaxation practice. It is not a substitute for medical or mental health care. Please talk with your doctor about any health concerns.

Can I bring a friend or my family?

Yes, for group meditations and holiday photo sessions. Ask when you book.

Is it safe to be near horses?

Tiffany's horses are calm and well known to her, but horses are large animals and can be unpredictable. You will follow simple safety instructions and sign a waiver.
